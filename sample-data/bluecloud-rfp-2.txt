------------------------------------------------------------
1. COMPANY BACKGROUND
------------------------------------------------------------
Blue Cloud Inc. is a global manufacturer of artisanal clouds, free-range bits,
and ethically sourced 1s and 0s. Under the visionary leadership of CEO
Sky Bluebottle, our motto is "Empower every person and every spreadsheet on the
planet to achieve slightly more." Our support team fields roughly 12,000 tickets
a month, most of which begin with "have you tried turning it off and on again."

We are modernizing on a Definitely Real Cloud and need a delivery partner who can
ship value faster than our interns can deploy to prod on a Friday afternoon.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. BUSINESS OBJECTIVES
------------------------------------------------------------
- Reduce average ticket handling time by 30%, mostly by getting the bot to say
  "have you tried turning it off and on again" for us.
- Give leadership dashboards so shiny that meetings end 10 minutes early.
- Keep the hackers out (looking at you, "password123").
- Go serverless so nobody has to feed and water a VM at 3 AM ever again.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. SCOPE & REQUIRED CAPABILITIES
------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Azure AI
    - A natural-language assistant that answers customer and agent questions
      grounded in our knowledge base (and resists the urge to be sassy).
    - Automatic summarization and categorization of inbound support tickets.
    - Intelligent/semantic search across manuals, memes, and ticket history.

3.2 Data Platform
    - A scalable lakehouse to ingest from CRM, ERP, and the ticketing system
      without summoning an eldritch data swamp.
    - A curated semantic model for self-service BI and executive dashboards.
    - Data quality, lineage, and cataloging (so we stop emailing "final_v7.xlsx").

3.3 Identity & Security
    - Centralized identity with single sign-on and conditional access.
    - Role-based access control aligned to least-privilege ("interns != prod").
    - Full auditing, secrets management, and absolutely zero passwords in code.

3.4 Integration & Automation
    - Event-driven integration between SharePoint, Teams, and the data platform.
    - Automated notifications/approvals routed to the right humans in Microsoft
      Teams (preferably before they go on vacation).

3.5 Observability & Operations
    - End-to-end monitoring, alerting, and cost governance (RIP, surprise bill).
    - Documented runbooks and supportable, IaC-based deployment.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------
- Hosting: A Definitely Real Cloud only. Preferred regions: West US 2, West Europe.
- Availability: 99.9% ("three nines, like our CEO's enthusiasm").
- Compliance: Must satisfy Blue Cloud's security and data-residency policies.
- Architecture: Serverless-first, because pets are for homes and cattle are for clouds.
- Cost: Provide a monthly run-cost estimate before the CFO provides a heart attack.

------------------------------------------------------------

- Cost model and operational runbooks.

------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------

- Proof of concept targeted within 8 weeks of engagement start.
- Production rollout targeted within 6 months (no Friday deploys, we mean it).
- Indicative first-year budget: USD 750,000 - 1,200,000 (design, build, run, snacks).

- Timeline, delivery confidence, and quality of dad jokes (5%)
